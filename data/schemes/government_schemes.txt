GOVERNMENT AGRICULTURE SCHEMES

Government agriculture schemes can provide farmers with different types of support such as income assistance, crop insurance, credit, irrigation support, infrastructure support, and other agricultural services.

1. PM-KISAN

PM-KISAN is a Government of India income-support scheme for eligible landholding farmer families, subject to the applicable scheme rules.

Farmers should check the latest eligibility requirements and application or beneficiary-status information through official government sources.

2. Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

PMFBY is a crop insurance scheme intended to provide financial protection to eligible farmers against specified crop losses and risks covered under the scheme.

Coverage, premium, eligibility, and claim conditions depend on the applicable notification and crop/area.

3. Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY)

PMKSY focuses on improving irrigation and water-use efficiency in agriculture.

Different components of the programme support irrigation development and efficient use of water.

4. Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

The Kisan Credit Card system provides eligible farmers access to agricultural credit through participating financial institutions, subject to applicable rules and eligibility conditions.

5. Soil Health Card

The Soil Health Card programme provides farmers with information about soil nutrient status and recommendations that can help guide nutrient management.

IMPORTANT:
Government schemes, eligibility requirements, application procedures, benefits, deadlines, and participating institutions can change.

The AI agriculture assistant should not claim that a farmer is eligible for a scheme without checking the latest official information.

Farmers should verify current scheme information through official government portals, agriculture departments, banks, or other authorized sources before applying.
