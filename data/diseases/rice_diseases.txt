RICE CROP DISEASES

1. Rice Blast

Rice blast is a common fungal disease of rice.

Symptoms:
- Gray or whitish lesions may appear on leaves.
- Lesions can develop into larger spots.
- The disease can affect different parts of the rice plant.

General management:
- Use healthy and suitable planting material.
- Avoid excessive nitrogen fertilizer.
- Maintain proper field and water management.
- Use disease-resistant rice varieties where available.
- For chemical treatment, farmers should follow the product label and local agricultural recommendations.

2. Bacterial Leaf Blight

Bacterial leaf blight is a bacterial disease affecting rice.

Symptoms:
- Long yellowish to whitish streaks may appear along leaf margins.
- In severe cases, leaves may dry.
- Young plants can be seriously affected.

General management:
- Use resistant varieties when available.
- Maintain proper water management.
- Avoid excessive nitrogen application.
- Remove severely affected plant material where appropriate.
- Follow local agricultural recommendations for disease management.

3. Sheath Blight

Sheath blight is a fungal disease that commonly affects the leaf sheaths of rice.

Symptoms:
- Greenish-gray or brown lesions may appear on leaf sheaths.
- Lesions can spread upward.
- Severe infection can reduce plant growth and yield.

General management:
- Avoid excessive nitrogen fertilizer.
- Maintain suitable plant spacing.
- Keep the field properly managed.
- Use recommended disease-management practices.

IMPORTANT:
Farmers should correctly identify the disease before applying pesticides or fungicides. Chemical products must be used only according to their label instructions and local agricultural recommendations.
