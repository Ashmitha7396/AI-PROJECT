FERTILIZER GUIDE FOR FARMERS

Fertilizers provide nutrients that help crops grow.

1. Nitrogen Fertilizers

Nitrogen supports leaf and vegetative growth.

Common nitrogen fertilizers include:
- Urea
- Ammonium sulfate

Farmers should avoid excessive nitrogen because it can cause excessive vegetative growth and may increase the risk of some crop diseases.

2. Phosphorus Fertilizers

Phosphorus supports root development and helps plants during early growth.

Examples include:
- DAP (Diammonium Phosphate)
- Single Super Phosphate (SSP)

3. Potassium Fertilizers

Potassium supports overall plant strength and can help crops tolerate environmental stress.

A common potassium fertilizer is:
- MOP (Muriate of Potash)

4. NPK Fertilizers

NPK fertilizers contain nitrogen (N), phosphorus (P), and potassium (K).

Different NPK formulations contain different proportions of these nutrients.

5. Important Safety Guidance

The correct fertilizer and application rate depend on the crop, soil condition, growth stage, and local recommendations.

Farmers should preferably use soil-test results and recommendations from qualified agricultural experts before applying fertilizers.

Do not apply fertilizer only because another farmer uses it. Nutrient requirements can differ between crops and fields.
