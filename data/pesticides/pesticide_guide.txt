PESTICIDE GUIDE FOR FARMERS

Pesticides are substances used to control pests that can damage crops.

1. Types of Pest Control Products

Insecticides:
Used to control insect pests.

Fungicides:
Used to control fungal diseases.

Herbicides:
Used to control unwanted weeds.

2. Before Using a Pesticide

Farmers should:
- Correctly identify the pest or disease.
- Check whether chemical treatment is actually necessary.
- Select a product that is approved and appropriate for the crop and target pest.
- Read the product label carefully.
- Follow the recommended application rate and safety instructions.
- Check the waiting or pre-harvest period when applicable.

3. Safe Pesticide Use

Farmers should:
- Wear the protective equipment specified on the product label.
- Avoid contact with skin and eyes.
- Avoid eating, drinking, or smoking while handling pesticides.
- Keep pesticides away from children and animals.
- Store products safely according to the label.
- Dispose of containers according to local regulations.

4. Integrated Pest Management

Integrated Pest Management (IPM) combines different methods of pest control.

These can include:
- Monitoring pest populations.
- Using resistant crop varieties.
- Maintaining field hygiene.
- Using biological control methods where suitable.
- Using pesticides only when necessary and according to recommendations.

5. Important Warning

The correct pesticide, dose, concentration, and application frequency depend on the crop, pest, product formulation, local regulations, and label instructions.

The AI agriculture assistant should NOT invent pesticide doses, prices, or product recommendations.

Farmers should follow the product label and consult qualified agricultural experts or local agriculture authorities when necessary.
